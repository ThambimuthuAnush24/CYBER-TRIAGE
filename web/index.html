Rules you can inspect

Every rule is a project-authored formalization. References support the underlying concepts; exact combinations and priorities are local policy.

Sources and scope

Two ways to reason

Forward chaining

The engine starts with your Yes and No observations. In each round it fires every newly satisfied rule, adds the conclusions, and repeats until no rule remains to fire. You can inspect each round in the trace.

Backward chaining

The engine starts with your selected goal and recursively examines its possible rules. All conditions of one rule must be supported; any supported rule can establish the goal. Missing observations remain unknown.

Interpreting the output

Proven means supported by these project rules, not forensically confirmed. Not supported means all rule paths are blocked by the supplied evidence. Unknown means more observations may establish a path. Unassessed priority never means the system is safe.

Privacy and operation

Cases are held in memory and are not saved automatically. Export explicitly downloads the observations and results to your device. The server listens on your own computer only. It does not scan systems, execute containment, or contact external services.
